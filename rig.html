CMD, commander: picks bounds, signals UAV, drone operator: launches from his pack, stays at the controls MG, gunner: holds cover, suppresses R1, rifleman with the commander (team A) R2, rifleman with the gunner (team B)

Bounding overwatch: one team moves to cover nearer the threat while the other covers it, then they swap. Dashed lines are planned routes, solid lines are trails, rings are chosen cover. On contact, when the threat moves and when a man is down, the radio man calls it in and team Bravo (on the flank) answers.

Playing a clip pauses the squad AI. Grenade throws at the threat; Radio keys the chest radio. Moving clips (walk, run, aim-walk, crawl, swim) send the soldier round a patrol loop. Once-only clips (reload, jump, signals, hit) return to the previous state.

Code API 
const e = Enemies.spawn({ position:[2,0,0] });
e.play('reload');      // any clip: radio,
                       // throwGrenade, crawl...
e.damage(25, 'head');  // zone multiplier
e.detach('helmet');    // a physics body
e.fig.parts.rifle / .helmet / .pack / .mag
Enemies.squads[0].setThreat([0,0,-8]);

Changes apply to every soldier. Clicks register on these cylinders: a headshot (×4) kills in one hit.

Selected soldier's separate objects

The person, rifle (with magazine), helmet and backpack are separate, closed objects on their own bones (without the helmet you see his hair). Drop the rifle and he draws the compact carbine from his back; with that gone too, he runs back for the rifle. The flame trooper fires only standing. Detached, each becomes a rigid body with gravity, bounce and friction that collides with the ground, walls and crates.
